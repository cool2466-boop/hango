English

1. What we collect

  • Automatically: access logs — a one-way hashed form of your IP address (we do not store your real IP), your browser type, the page that referred you, and how you arrived (campaign links). Your quiz answers and your result score.

2. Why we use it

  • To show and send your diagnostic result and related Korean-study information.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information and do not share it with third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your information only while it is needed for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it. To unsubscribe or have your data deleted, email cool2466@gmail.com and we will handle it without delay.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
