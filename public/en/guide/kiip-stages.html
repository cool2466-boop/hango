KIIP stages and Korean requirements

KIIP runs from Stage 0 to Stage 5. Stages 1–4 are Korean language and culture classes; Stage 5 is Understanding Korean Society. You take a pre-test first, and that decides which stage you start at.

The stages

StageContentRough level
Stage 0Basic KoreanHangul, basic phrases
Stage 1Beginner 1~ TOPIK Level 1
Stage 2Beginner 2~ TOPIK Level 2
Stage 3Intermediate 1~ TOPIK Level 3
Stage 4Intermediate 2~ TOPIK Level 4
Stage 5Understanding Korean Society (basic / advanced)Residency / naturalisation

The pre-test and TOPIK links

When you start KIIP you take a pre-test. Your score decides your starting stage. People who already hold a TOPIK grade may be placed at the matching stage or exempted from some stages (check the current linking rules).

Why do KIIP?

  • Meets the Korean and society requirements for permanent residency (F-5) and naturalisation
  • Accepted as the Korean requirement for E-7-4 instead of TOPIK
  • A structured curriculum to follow

Class places are limited by region, so there can be competition to enrol. Applications are made at socinet.go.kr.

Stage structure, hours, assessments and TOPIK linking can change. Check socinet.go.kr for the latest.
