TOPIK Level 2: what score do you need?

TOPIK I is scored out of 200 points, and the Level 2 pass mark is 140. 80 or more is Level 1; 140 or more is Level 2. There is no minimum per section — only the total counts.

How the score works

SectionQuestionsPoints
Listening30100
Reading40100
Total70200

TOPIK I has no writing section. Writing only appears in TOPIK II.

Grade cutoffs

GradeTotal (out of 200)
Level 180+
Level 2140+

So Level 2 means clearing 140 out of 200 (70%). If one section is weaker, a stronger score in the other can still carry you over the line.

Why Level 2 matters

TOPIK Level 2 is used as the Korean-ability benchmark in several visa and residency steps — most notably the E-7-4 skilled worker visa and the Social Integration Program (KIIP).

Format and scoring follow the official announcements at topik.go.kr and can change. Check the official notice before you register.
