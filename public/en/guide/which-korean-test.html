Which Korean test do you need?

TOPIK, EPS-TOPIK and KIIP (Social Integration Program) sound similar but are separate systems with different purposes. What you need right now decides which one applies to you.

Side by side

TOPIKEPS-TOPIKKIIP
Run byNIIEDHRD KoreaMinistry of Justice
PurposeGeneral Korean proficiency (study, work, visa)Selecting E-9 workersIntegration / residency & naturalisation course
Mainly forAnyoneApplicants coming on E-9Foreigners already living in Korea
FormatExam (several times a year, IBT/paper)Exam (before entry, mostly overseas)Course attendance + stage tests

Pick by situation

  • Already working in Korea, preparing for E-7-4 / residency / naturalisation → TOPIK or KIIP. Both are often accepted as the Korean requirement.
  • Not yet in Korea, applying for an E-9 job → EPS-TOPIK.
  • University entry or graduation requirement → TOPIK (usually TOPIK II).

For most workers already in Korea, what matters in practice is TOPIK Level 2 or KIIP Stage 2.

Requirements change. Check TOPIK at topik.go.kr, EPS-TOPIK at eps.hrdkorea.or.kr, KIIP at socinet.go.kr, and visa rules at hikorea.go.kr.
