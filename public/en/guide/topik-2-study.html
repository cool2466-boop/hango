How to study for TOPIK Level 2 on your own

TOPIK Level 2 is 140 out of 200 on TOPIK I. Tidy up beginner vocabulary and grammar, get used to the question types, and it is a realistic self-study goal.

What to study

  • Vocabulary: around 1,500–2,000 basic words for daily life, work and public situations
  • Grammar: particles, tenses, connectors (-아서/어서, -지만, -(으)면), honorifics and other beginner patterns
  • Question types: reading notices, text messages and short passages; ordering sentences; choosing the matching statement

By section

Listening (100 points)

Many questions ask for the situation or purpose of a short dialogue. Listen to beginner audio for 10–15 minutes daily and check anything you miss against the script.

Reading (100 points)

Practical texts — signs, ads, notices — are the core. Practise finding the answer from overall context even when some words are unknown.

Free resources

  • NIIED TOPIK site (topik.go.kr) — some past papers are released
  • HRD Korea EPS-TOPIK public question book — useful for beginner vocabulary and expressions
  • King Sejong Institute online (Nuri-Sejonghakdang) — free beginner Korean lessons
